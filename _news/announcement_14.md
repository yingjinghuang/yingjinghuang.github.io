The submission deadline for the [1st International Workshop on Geography According to Foundation Models (GeoFM 2026)](https://stko-lab.github.io/agile2026-geofm/) has been officially extended.

If you are working on late-breaking results or conceptual papers on GeoAI ethics and foundation models, this is your chance to join us in Tartu. We are looking for short papers of 4 to 6 pages using the CEURART template.

- 📅 **New submission deadline:** May 8, 2026
- 📅 **Workshop date:** June 16, 2026, Tartu, Estonia
- 👉 [Workshop website and submission details](https://stko-lab.github.io/agile2026-geofm/)
